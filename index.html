Simple when every round is the same. Advanced when it isn't.

Simple. Five numbers.

Work, rest, exercises, rounds, and rest between rounds. Set them and the total time is right there. Tabata, circuits, boxing rounds, interval runs.

Advanced. Every interval.

Each exercise gets its own work and rest. Duplicate a round, drag one into place, name it. Diamond push-ups get a full minute of rest. They earned it.
