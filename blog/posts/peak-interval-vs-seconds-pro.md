If you are comparing Peak Interval vs Seconds Pro, the honest answer is that they are not trying to win in exactly the same way.

Seconds Pro is often the app people associate with power and depth. Peak Interval is stronger when you care about speed, cleaner workflow, and a more modern path from workout idea to actual training.

## Choose Peak Interval if...

Peak Interval is probably the better fit if you want:

- faster workout creation
- a cleaner iPhone plus Apple Watch experience
- less friction when turning saved workouts into timers
- a more modern feel

That is especially true if you regularly build workouts from screenshots, social posts, or coach-written plans and want the app to help you move faster.

## Choose Seconds Pro if...

Seconds Pro may still be the better fit if your top priority is extremely deep timer logic and you already rely on a long-established advanced setup.

If you are a heavy power user and your current workflow is perfect, there may be no reason to switch.

## The real tradeoff

This comparison is less about "which app is objectively better?" and more about "what kind of user are you?"

If you want maximum configuration depth, Seconds Pro still has a strong case.

If you want the fastest route from workout structure to a timer you can actually run, Peak Interval has the stronger case.

## Bottom line

Peak Interval vs Seconds Pro comes down to complexity versus speed. For many users, especially people who value Apple Watch flow and fast workout setup, Peak Interval is the more practical choice.
