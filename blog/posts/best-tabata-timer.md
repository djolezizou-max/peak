The best Tabata timer should do one thing extremely well: make short, intense intervals feel effortless to follow.

Tabata sounds simple because it is simple. Twenty seconds on, ten seconds off, repeated across rounds. But when the effort is high, clarity matters more than ever. You should not be guessing where you are in the workout.

## What matters in a Tabata timer

For Tabata, the essentials are:

- precise 20/10 timing
- clear round cues
- easy repeatability
- fast setup
- the ability to extend beyond a single 8-round block if needed

Most people do not need a complicated experience for Tabata. They need a timer that disappears into the workout.
