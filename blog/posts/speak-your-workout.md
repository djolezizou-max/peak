You already know the workout. Five rounds, four exercises, 30 seconds on, 20 off, then the names of the moves. Typing that in from the floor takes longer than the first round, and you lose the count while you are still in the builder.

In Peak Interval, on iPhone or Android, go to Import, then Speak your workout. It starts listening right away. The line on the screen is the example: "5 rounds, 4 exercises, 30 seconds on, 20 off". Say something in that shape, then name the exercises.

## Say the structure, then the names

Give it the rounds, how many exercises, the work, and the rest. Then name the moves, in order. The preview builds live as you talk, so you can see the workout before you have finished the sentence.

Anything you leave unsaid is filled in. The defaults are 30 seconds of work, 20 seconds of rest, and 60 seconds between rounds. Those fills belong to this builder. A follow-along import from an Instagram or TikTok post uses 15 seconds of rest when the post never states one, which is a different path, in [turn an Instagram or TikTok workout into a follow-along timer](/blog/posts/import-instagram-tiktok-workout). A photo of a whiteboard is the third path, in [how to turn a workout screenshot into an interval timer](/blog/posts/import-workout-from-screenshot).

## Change it without starting over

Keep talking. A correction, a reorder, "make it 3 rounds", and "scrap that, start over" all land on the preview you are looking at. You do not have to delete the workout and open the builder again.

When the preview matches what you meant, say "that's all" or tap the checkmark. That is the end of the dictation. The workout on the screen is the one you just described.

## Any language, including a mix

You can speak any language, and you can mix languages in one workout. Names stay in the language you spoke. A move you called "sentadilla" stays "sentadilla". It is not rewritten as squat, and a round you named in English stays in English if that is how you said it.

Mixing inside one workout is fine. The preview keeps each name as you said it, in the language you used for that name.

## What this is, and what it is not

The limits are 20 rounds and 50 exercises per round. There is no free-plan cap on building a workout by voice. Saving is limited to 1 workout on the free plan. Premium is unlimited.

This is not Voice Cues. Voice Cues, including the celebrity voice packs, are what the timer says once the workout exists: the next exercise, the countdown, the halfway word. How to set those is in [interval timer with voice cues](/blog/posts/interval-timer-with-voice-coach). Speak your workout is how the intervals get built.

Say the example, name the moves, fix the one thing you said wrong, and say "that's all". The preview is the workout. You do not type it in a second time.
