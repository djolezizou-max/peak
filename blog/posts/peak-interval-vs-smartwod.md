Peak Interval vs SmartWOD is really a question about workflow.

Both can serve interval-style training, but they appeal to slightly different users. If your priority is getting custom workouts into a timer quickly and running them cleanly on iPhone and Apple Watch, Peak Interval has a strong edge.

## Choose Peak Interval if...

Peak Interval is better if you want:

- fast custom workout creation
- repeatable saved workouts
- strong iPhone and Apple Watch flow
- a practical way to turn found workouts into real timers

That makes it especially useful for people who collect workouts from creators, classes, notes, or coaches and want to execute them with less setup friction.

## Choose SmartWOD if...

If you already live inside a SmartWOD-style training flow and it matches your habits well, sticking with it can make sense. The best app is often the one that fits your workflow without resistance.

## The real difference

The difference is not just features. It is emphasis.

Peak Interval is built around turning workout structure into execution quickly. If that is your bottleneck, it is the better bet.

## Bottom line

Peak Interval vs SmartWOD comes down to whether you want the app to feel faster and more flexible for custom interval building. If you do, Peak Interval is the stronger option.
